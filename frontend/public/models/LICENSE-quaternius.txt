The men's pack is mirrored as .gltf with the buffer inlined as base64. It was
repacked to .glb losslessly (JSON chunk + decoded BIN chunk, no re-encode, no
asset changes) so both packs load through the same GLTFLoader path.

Contents of every file: one 62-bone humanoid rig, 24 animation clips
(Idle, Idle_Neutral, Interact, Walk, Run, Wave, ...), flat-colour materials and
no textures, 1.5–1.8 MB each.

Modifications made in this project (all at runtime, the .glb files are
untouched): per-agent material colour overrides, a seated pose built by
rotating hip/knee/shoulder bones, and optional hijab / glasses / headphone
geometry parented to the Head bone.
